3. What Mini Keyboard is (and is not)

Mini Keyboard turns your iPhone into a keyboard (and, with the optional unlock, a trackpad) for your own Mac. After you pair the two apps by scanning a QR code shown on the Mac, keystrokes, pointer movement, scrolling and clicks travel directly from the iPhone to the Mac over your own local network, across an encrypted connection. There is no server in between: nothing you type passes through us, and the apps do not work across the internet — both devices must be on your own network.

Because input is delivered to the Mac exactly as if typed locally, Mini Keyboard is not a remote administration, monitoring or parental-control product, and it deliberately has no features for observing a Mac's screen or activity. It is an input device. The quality of the connection depends on your network; interference, congestion or OS-level network permissions can interrupt it.

4. No account required

There is no sign-up, no email address, and no login. Pairing state lives on your own devices (the Mac keeps the pairing secret in its Keychain), and choosing a new pairing code replaces it. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what the apps do and do not collect.

5. Purchases — the Trackpad unlock

Typing with Mini Keyboard is free and is never gated. The app offers a single optional in-app purchase, the Trackpad unlock, which enables the pull-up and full-screen trackpad. It is a one-time, non-consumable purchase — not a subscription: it never renews, and there is nothing to cancel. The price is shown in the app before you buy and is charged by Apple to your Apple ID. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID and is restored automatically on any device signed into it; a Restore Purchases button is also available in the app. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's own policies — we cannot issue them directly; request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
