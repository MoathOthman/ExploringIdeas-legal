Mini Keyboard — two-device demo

A physical iPhone pairing with a Mac over local Wi-Fi, then typing to it live.

Recorded on camera so both devices are visible in one continuous shot. Download the file.

What the recording shows

  1. Pairing. The iPhone finds the Mac on the same Wi-Fi network and connects — the status line reads Live to …'s MacBook Pro.
  2. Typing. Characters typed on the iPhone keyboard appear on the Mac as real keystrokes, in whichever app has focus.
  3. The trackpad. The on-screen trackpad moves the Mac's pointer.

Why a video

Mini Keyboard is an input device: the iPhone app is only half of it, and the other half is the companion Mac app on a separate machine. Nothing in the iPhone app can be demonstrated on its own, so the pair has to be filmed together.

The Mac app is free on the Mac App Store and both sides must be on the same local network. No account, no server, no internet connection is involved — the two devices talk directly to each other.
