FAQ

Email dark2torch@gmail.com for anything not covered below.

Is my data safe?

Your documents are processed and stored only on your device and are never uploaded. There is no account. FairScan does send anonymous usage analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase), a push-notification token, purchase status (Apple and RevenueCat) and feedback only if you choose to send it. None of that includes your documents, their text or their names. Read the full Privacy Policy.

What happens after 10 documents?

Everything you've saved stays yours and stays readable. To add more documents, delete some or upgrade to FairScan Pro.

Is there a free trial?

No. The free tier (10 documents) is how you try FairScan. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase or a monthly or yearly subscription, and you only pay when you choose to.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose FairScan. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. The lifetime purchase is not a subscription and never renews.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the upgrade screen in FairScan and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with.

What about e-sign?

FairScan Pro lets you draw a signature and place it on a document for personal use. For legally binding e-signatures with audit trails, you will need a dedicated signing service.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Scanning, text recognition, folders and PDF creation all work offline. Buying or restoring Pro needs an internet connection.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly.
