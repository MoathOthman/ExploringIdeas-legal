Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

The short version: Rowmend cleans your spreadsheets entirely on your Mac. Your rows — every cell, name, email, and number — are read, transformed, and exported locally and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics service phoning home — this version of the app collects no data at all.

Your spreadsheet data stays on your Mac

Rowmend is a local-first CSV and spreadsheet cleaner. When you open a file, Rowmend reads and parses it on your Mac and holds it in memory while you work; applying a cleaning recipe, previewing the before/after diff, and exporting the result all happen locally. Your data rows never leave your device and are never transmitted to us or anyone else. There is no Rowmend account, no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync.

How Rowmend opens and saves your files

Rowmend only ever touches a file you explicitly choose through the standard macOS open panel (or by dragging it in). To let you reopen a recent file quickly, macOS gives the app a security-scoped bookmark — a local reference to that file's location on your own disk. The bookmark is not a copy of your data and is not sent anywhere. Exports are files you save, where you choose, through the standard macOS save panel.

No data collected — the honest disclosure

This version of Rowmend transmits nothing off your Mac. No analytics events, no crash reports to our servers, no telemetry, no identifiers. The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected," and that is literally true.

AI-assisted recipes and the "rows never leave your Mac" rule

Rowmend turns a plain-English goal into a cleaning recipe. In this version, that suggestion runs entirely on your device using a built-in, rule-based generator — nothing is sent anywhere.

A future update may add an optional, AI-assisted suggestion mode. It is designed around one strict rule: it would send only your column header names and the goal you type — the schema of your table — to generate a recipe. It would never send your data rows or cell values. If and when that mode ships, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy label first, and describe exactly what it sends. Either way, the transform engine that actually changes your data always runs locally on your Mac.

What Rowmend does not do

To be explicit:

  • We do not transmit your spreadsheets, rows, column values, or file contents to any server.
  • We do not use an advertising identifier and show no ads — there is no third-party advertising or analytics SDK in the app.
  • We do not track you across apps, websites, or companies, and we do not use App Tracking Transparency because there is no tracking of any kind.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, location, or photo library. Rowmend only reads the specific files you open.
  • Rowmend may check whether our other apps are installed, to show an optional "More Apps" suggestion, using Apple's canOpenURL. That is a local check on your own device and sends nothing to us.
  • We do not sell or share your data — we never have it in the first place.

Saved recipes and preferences

Recipes you save and your app preferences are stored on your Mac using Apple's SwiftData and the system defaults. A recipe is a set of cleaning steps (and may reference your column header names by text); it does not contain your data rows. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Purchases

Any optional purchase in Rowmend (Rowmend Pro) is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Rowmend never sees your payment method, Apple ID, or billing details — Apple processes the payment and we never receive card data. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

No account required

Rowmend requires no sign-up, no email address, and no login to work. There is no Rowmend account and no Rowmend server that stores your identity or your files.

Children

Rowmend is a productivity utility, is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal data from anyone of any age.

Data breach risk

Because your files and rows are processed only on your Mac and are never uploaded, there is no Rowmend database of your data to breach. If your device is compromised, that is governed by Apple's device security model — we recommend a strong login password and FileVault.

Retention

Rowmend keeps no copy of your data. Your saved recipes and preferences live only on your Mac and are removed when you delete them or uninstall the app. The files you clean are your own; keep your own backups of anything important.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the App Store release notes. Continued use of Rowmend after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
