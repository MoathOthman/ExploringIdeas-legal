Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version: What you and your partner write in Kindling — answers, letters, notes, dates — is stored on your phone and is never sent to us. There is no account and no login. Photos and videos you pick for a game are read on your phone and never sent to us; to draw the map, Kindling asks Apple Maps, so Apple receives the place. Kindling also sends diagnostics and usage events, and, if you allow notifications, a notification address for your phone. These are tied to a random identifier made for this installation, not to your name. No third-party ads, no tracking across other companies' apps, and we do not sell data.

What stays on your phone

Kindling is played on one shared phone. These are saved on that phone and are never sent to us:

  • Your answers and your answer history, including the text of the question you answered.
  • Time capsule letters and appreciation jar notes.
  • Anniversaries and other dates you enter.
  • Your streak, your game results and your monthly recap.
  • Your settings, such as appearance and the reminder time.

This is deleted when you delete Kindling. We have no copy, so we cannot restore it and we cannot read it. If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, Apple includes app data in that backup; that backup is between you and Apple.

Photos, videos and location

The game Where & When asks you to pick photos or videos, and your partner guesses where and when each one was taken.

  • You pick each item in Apple's own photo picker. Kindling sees only the items you pick, not the rest of your library, and does not ask for access to your library.
  • Kindling reads the place and date saved inside each picked photo or video. This happens on your phone.
  • The picked items are copied into a temporary folder for the round and deleted when the round ends. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The map, the place search and the place name are provided by Apple Maps. To do this, your phone sends Apple the area of the map you are looking at, the text you type in the search box, and the coordinates of the photo whose place is being named. Apple handles this under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive it.

Kindling does not ask for your current location, and does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or health data.

What leaves your phone

Kindling creates a random identifier for this installation. It is not your name, email address, phone number or Apple ID, and it is replaced if you delete and reinstall the app. The items below are tied to identifiers of that kind.

Diagnostics and usage. Kindling uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

  • Crash reports: device model, operating-system version, app version, and where in the code the problem happened.
  • Usage events: for example that a screen was opened, which pack or game was played, or that a purchase completed. These carry identifiers of packs, games and plans, and counts.

These never include your answers, your letters or notes, the text of a question, your photos or videos, or a location. Google handles this data as described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

Notifications. Kindling asks for permission before it sends any notification.

  • Your nightly reminder and time capsule reminders are scheduled by your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.
  • Announcements are messages we send, for example about a new pack. The screen that asks for notification permission says so. If you allow notifications, announcements are on, and you can turn them off at any time in Kindling's Settings.
  • To deliver notifications, your phone's notification address (a push token) is registered with Apple, with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, and with a server we operate. With it we store: the random identifier, the app version, the operating-system version, your phone's language and region setting, its time zone, whether notifications are allowed, and whether announcements are on.

Messages inside the app. Kindling asks our server whether there is a message to show, sending the app's name and the random identifier. It reports when the app was opened and whether a message was shown, tapped or closed.

Purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment method, your Apple ID or your billing details. Kindling uses RevenueCat to confirm what you have bought; RevenueCat receives a random identifier and your purchase history for Kindling, under RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Feedback you choose to send. If you use “Suggest a Feature”, we receive what you type, an email address only if you enter one, a price only if you suggest one, and: the app version, device model, operating-system version, language and region setting, the random identifier, and whether you have Pro. Please do not put anything private in feedback.

A question when you cancel. If you turn off renewal of a subscription, Kindling may ask once why. If you answer, we receive the reason you chose, the plan, how many days you had it, the app version, and your language and region setting.

Our other apps. Kindling sometimes suggests another app we make. To do so it checks whether that app is already on your phone, asks Apple's App Store for that app's public details, and reports to us whether a suggestion was shown or tapped.

Sharing. When you share a result card or send a question to someone, you choose what is sent and to whom. A shared question is a link that contains the question's number, not your answer.

What we do not do

  • We do not receive or store your answers, letters, notes, photos, videos or locations.
  • We do not require an account, an email address, a phone number or a name.
  • We do not show third-party ads, use the advertising identifier, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • We do not sell data or share it with data brokers.

Your choices

  • Notifications: turn them off for Kindling in your phone's Settings.
  • Announcements: turn them off in Kindling's Settings and keep your own reminder.
  • Everything on your phone: delete Kindling to remove it.
  • Feedback you sent, or any other record: email us and we will delete what we can find. We do not know who you are, so tell us what you sent and roughly when.

Children

Kindling is made for adult couples and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page. Material changes are noted in the App Store release notes.
