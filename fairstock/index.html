Support & FAQ

Need help? Email kenz@kenzapps.com — we aim to reply within two business days. Common questions below.

Is my inventory safe?

Yes. FairStock has no servers for your data and requires no account. Every item, quantity, price and photo is stored on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Does FairStock sync to the cloud?

Not in this version — there is no cloud copy at all. Use Back up in Settings to export a single backup file, and Restore to bring it onto a new device. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.

Is "unlimited items free" for real?

Yes. There is no cap on items, photos or folders, and no trial that expires. Pro adds automation and professional output — alerts, reports, more label templates, multiple sites — with one pay-once price.

Do I need special label paper?

No. The Standard sheet is laid out for common 30-up address-label paper, and single labels print on anything. Pro adds shelf tags, price labels and a custom grid for other papers.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. Scanning, counting, labels, reports and exports all work offline. Your data is processed on your device, so no connection is needed.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for any in-app purchase are handled by Apple — visit reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no mechanism to issue refunds directly.
