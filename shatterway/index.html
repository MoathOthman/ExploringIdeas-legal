Shatterway

Tap. Shatter. Keep moving.

One rule makes this a game. You only have so many balls. Miss, and that one is gone. Let a pane of glass reach you, and it costs you ten. Crystals are the only way to get more.

How it plays

  • Tap to throw a steel ball at the point you touched.
  • Hold to enter Focus — time drops to a quarter speed while you aim, on a meter that drains as you hold it and refills when one ball breaks several.
  • Bank shots. Steel plates bounce your throws, so a pane behind a pillar is reachable off the plate beside it.
  • Forty levels, three stars each — finish it, let nothing touch you, take every crystal.

What it does not have

  • No ads.
  • No subscription.
  • No energy, no timers, no second currency.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Anonymous usage and crash data only — see the privacy policy.
